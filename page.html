The Charles Tochukwu Okey-Okoro Foundation is committed to bridging gaps in healthcare, social support, and humanitarian relief, ensuring that no one is left behind.

Join us Today

Every initiative, every donation, and every act of service fuels our mission of hope and lasting change.

The Charles Tochukwu Okey-Okoro Foundation was established to respond to the growing health, social, and humanitarian challenges faced by underserved communities. The foundation is committed to bridging the gaps in health education, preventive care, and access to basic necessities for vulnerable populations.

By organizing community outreach programs, blood drives, and advocacy efforts, the foundation works in partnership with government agencies, civil society organizations, and other stakeholders to create sustainable solutions.

Our Schematic Areas

Health & Well-Being Promotion

We are committed to advancing public health through education, awareness, and advocacy. This includes preventive healthcare campaigns, mental health awareness, capacity-building programs, and community outreach to empower individuals and families to live healthier lives.

Learn More

Humanitarian Support & Social Impact

We provide relief materials, targeted assistance, and interventions for vulnerable groups—including children, women, the elderly, and persons with disabilities—while fostering a culture of compassion and service within communities.

Learn More

Advocacy for Sustainable Change

We collaborate with government agencies, healthcare institutions, civil society, and private sector actors to organize blood drives, mobilize resources, and influence policies that address pressing health and social challenges in underserved communities.
